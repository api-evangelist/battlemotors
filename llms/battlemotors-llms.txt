# Battle Motors

> Battle Motors is an American manufacturer of purpose-built, severe-service vocational trucks engineered for refuse and recycling, infrastructure, ground support, agriculture, and oil & gas operations. The company designs and builds trucks on diesel, compressed natural gas (CNG), and battery-electric (BEV) platforms, paired with the Fortris™ Control Hub software for connected fleet operations.

Battle Motors is headquartered in New Philadelphia, Ohio, USA. The brand evolved from Crane Carrier Company (CCC), a heavy-truck manufacturer with roots dating to 1946. The current Battle Motors lineup centers on two purpose-built chassis families — LNT and Raider — available in cabover and conventional configurations across diesel, CNG, and electric drivetrains.

**Primary domains:** battlemotors.com (this site) and fortris.ai (Fortris Control Hub software).

## Key products

- [LNT Diesel](https://battlemotors.com/build/lnt-diesel): Cabover severe-service chassis with diesel powertrain. Industry-leading visibility, tight turning radius, ergonomic right-hand stand-up drive option for refuse routes.
- [LNT Non-CDL](https://battlemotors.com/build/lnt-non-cdl): LNT configured under CDL gross-weight thresholds for operators without a Commercial Driver's License.
- [LNT BEV (Electric)](https://battlemotors.com/build/lnt-bev): Battery-electric LNT for zero-tailpipe-emission refuse, recycling, and municipal routes.
- [Raider Diesel](https://battlemotors.com/build/raider): Conventional-cab severe-service chassis for infrastructure, construction, and heavy hauling.
- [Raider Crew Cab](https://battlemotors.com/build/raider-crew-cab): Raider with crew-cab configuration for utility, emergency, and field-service teams.
- [Raider CNG](https://battlemotors.com/build/raider-cng): Compressed-natural-gas Raider for low-emission vocational operations.
- [Raider BEV (Electric)](https://battlemotors.com/build/raider-bev): Battery-electric Raider for zero-tailpipe-emission vocational applications.
